STEP 01Discover

What is really in there

A deterministic scan of the legacy shop. Each touchpoint shows what it is today, what it becomes, and how sure the mapping is. Anything it cannot map goes to a human, never guessed.

STEP 02Rewrite

A diff a person can approve

Nothing is applied automatically. The migrated shop goes to a separate folder and every change is listed here for review.

STEP 03Check and repair

The AI got it wrong. Cutover caught it.

Real mistakes from real runs. Automatic checks found each one and sent the failure back to the model. The shop only moved on once every check was green.

STEP 04Prove

Proof, not trust

The same seven scenarios ran against the old and migrated shops in a real browser, as a sandbox buyer. After every payment, PayPal itself was asked what happened.

Built for the PayPal AI Hackathon. Re-run the proof yourself: node agent/migrate.mjs then node scripts/scenarios.mjs. See the README.
